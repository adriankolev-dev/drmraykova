Good preparation makes the visit more effective and calmer — for you and for the doctor.

## Before the appointment

- Book a convenient time and note the address.
- Bring ID and health documents as required.
- Take previous results, scans, and discharge summaries if you have them.
- Write down medications you take.

## Questions worth asking

- What does today's exam show?
- Are further tests needed?
- When should I come for the next check-up?
- Is there anything I should watch for until then?

## If you feel anxious

Say so at the beginning. Calm explanation of each step is part of quality care — especially at a first visit or after a long pause.

## Important reminder

Bring previous medical documentation. It saves time and supports a more accurate assessment.

This is an educational checklist. Do not delay care for acute symptoms.
