One of the most common questions is: “Should I see a gynecologist every year?” The short answer: frequency is individual.

## What affects the interval

- age and life stage;
- previous test results;
- current symptoms;
- family or personal history;
- recommendations after a specific visit.

For many women, a yearly check-up is a good guide, but your plan may be more or less frequent.

## When not to wait for the “regular” date

Seek care sooner for:

- strong or unusual pain;
- irregular or heavy bleeding;
- unusual discharge or irritation;
- concern after a test result.

## Practical tip

At the end of each visit, ask: “When should we meet again?” You get a clear personal guide instead of a generic rule.
