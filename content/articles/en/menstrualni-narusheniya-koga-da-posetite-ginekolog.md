Cycles are not identical for every woman, but some changes are worth discussing with a specialist.

## Signs to seek care

Book a visit for:

- very heavy or very light bleeding;
- a much longer or much shorter cycle;
- strong pain that interferes with daily life;
- bleeding between periods;
- a sudden change from your usual pattern.

## What helps the doctor

- an approximate calendar of recent cycles;
- a description of pain and bleeding intensity;
- a list of medications and contraception (if any);
- previous test results.

## What to expect

The visit starts with a conversation. A clinical exam may follow, and ultrasound or other tests when needed. The goal is to understand the cause and create a clear plan.

## Important

Not every irregularity means a serious problem, but self-assessment does not replace an exam. If in doubt — book an appointment.

This article is educational and does not provide a diagnosis.
